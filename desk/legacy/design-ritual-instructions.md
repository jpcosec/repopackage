# Design Ritual Instructions

## Mission
- Convert raw design input into durable drawer specs and promotion candidates.

## Inputs
- Design input package conforming to `contracts/design_input_contract.md`
- Source evidence such as conversations, transcripts, or notes

## Procedure
1. Validate the design input package.
2. Distill evidence into spec, decisions, open questions, and curated artifacts.
3. Store durable results in `desk/drawers/specs/<topic>/`.
4. Identify reusable pills and promotion candidates.
5. Record unresolved ambiguity explicitly.
6. Do not generate executable tasks until promotion criteria are satisfied.

## Outputs
- Drawer spec package
- Promotion candidate list
- Candidate pills

## Rules
- Do not skip evidence grounding.
- Do not promote ambiguous drawer material into execution.
- Prefer durable specs over temporary summaries.
